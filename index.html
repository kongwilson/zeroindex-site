How far are you from scratch?

ZeroIndex records every shot you play — one tap on your iPhone or Apple Watch — and measures how far each one went, in the conditions you played it in. Over your rounds it shows which strokes you can win back first.

  • Plays-like distances that count the slope, the wind and the temperature.
  • Your own shot scatter on the hole in front of you, to plan a good miss.
  • Australia's golf courses, listed from OpenStreetMap — and any without a hole layout, mapped by you on the satellite map in a few minutes.
  • Your data stays on your devices. No account, no ads, no tracking.
